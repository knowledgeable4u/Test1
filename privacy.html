Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Knowledgeable4u ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our content automation platform, including our integration with TikTok's Content Posting API.

1. Information We Collect

Our platform collects only the minimum information required to operate our TikTok content publishing automation:

  • TikTok OAuth tokens: When the account owner authorizes our app via TikTok's OAuth 2.0 flow, we receive and store an access token and refresh token. These are stored locally on our own server and are never shared with third parties.
  • TikTok profile information: We use the user.info.basic scope to retrieve the display name and open_id of the authenticated TikTok account solely to verify the identity of the account we are publishing to.
  • Publish logs: We store local logs of video uploads including publish IDs and timestamps for our own operational records.

2. How We Use the Information

We use the information collected solely for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate and maintain the connection between our automation system and our TikTok creator account.
  • To publish short-form educational video content to our own TikTok creator profile using the video.upload and video.publish scopes.
  • To verify that content has been successfully published by polling the TikTok publish status endpoint.

We do not use the information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating our own content publishing pipeline.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any user data to third parties. TikTok OAuth tokens and profile information are stored exclusively on our local server and are never transmitted to external services beyond TikTok's own API endpoints.

4. Single-Account Scope

This application is a private tool used exclusively by the owner of the Knowledgeable4u TikTok account. It is not a multi-user platform. No end-user data from third parties is collected or processed.

5. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are retained locally for as long as they remain valid or until the account owner revokes access. Publish logs are kept for operational purposes and can be deleted at any time at the account owner's request.

6. Security

All credentials and tokens are stored locally on a private server. We use HTTPS for all API communications with TikTok. We do not store credentials in public repositories or cloud services.

7. Your Rights

As this platform operates solely on behalf of the account owner, any request to access, modify, or delete associated data can be directed to us at the contact address below. You may also revoke our app's access at any time through your TikTok account settings under "Manage app permissions."

8. TikTok's Privacy Policy

Our use of TikTok's API is governed by TikTok's Partner Privacy Policy and TikTok's Developer Terms of Service.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects when the most recent changes were made.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: knowledgeable4u@gmail.com
